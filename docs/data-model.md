# Dataverse data model

Status: **Partially implemented**. Configured reference tables are distinguished below from proposed business entities. This reflects the development steps described during setup; it is not a production deployment or an independent audit of a solution export.

Custom schemas use publisher prefix `fmg`. Do not enter the prefix twice where Power Apps adds it automatically. Primary IDs, ownership, creation/modification timestamps, and system user references are generated by Dataverse.

## MVP boundary

The initial release supports **expense payments, budgets, spending analysis, approvals, and auditability**. Income, receipts, sales, invoicing, bank transfers, and other cash inflows are **not** part of this MVP. A later receipts module requires its own approved scope and data model; it must not be forced into the existing expense taxonomy.

Posted payment totals are cash-outflow management measures, not automatically accrual-basis expenses. The application cannot derive revenue, profit, margins, net invoicing, complete net cash flow, or real account balances from expense payments alone. The 100–900 codes are an internal management taxonomy, not a mapping to the Peruvian PCGE.

### Category rules

- The codes are **text values with numeric labels**, using the existing `100`, `200`, ... `900` groups and their numerical subcodes. Do not add `R` prefixes or introduce receipt categories.
- Group records have `IsGroup = true` and no parent. Leaf records have `IsGroup = false` and their group's `Parent Category` lookup.
- `fmg_parentcode` is the source/import reference; `fmg_parentcategory` is the relational parent. Both must remain consistent until the former is deliberately retired.
- `fmg_description` is a business description, not a transaction direction.
- **No `Direction` column exists in the implemented Category schema.** Specifically, do not assume `fmg_direction`, `fmg_defaultflowclass`, or `fmg_isbudgetable` has been created.
- `999` (`No Definido`) is allowed for incomplete drafts only, never for posted payments.
- The manual `Initialize Category Hierarchy` flow was used to assign parent lookups. Treat it as a one-time migration: validate all assignments and disable the initializer after use.
- Referenced categories must be deactivated instead of deleted. Renumbering requires a controlled migration with historical review.

### Cash Account rules

`Type` is a single-choice field with labels `Cash`, `Bank`, `Wallet`, and `Other`. Dataverse generates internal numeric values. Whether its choice definition is local or global does not change its use.

Development test data consists of two branch-associated cash boxes and one shared bank-account label without a Branch lookup. A blank account Branch does not mean a missing record or authorize unrestricted use. These are **internal labels**, not connected bank accounts or verified balances. No credentials, account numbers, or real balances belong in the repository.

**Do not create `Direction`, `Receipt`, or any receipt-specific flow class in the MVP.** A `Posted` record means an expense payment was made; drafts, unpaid purchases, and commitments are not payments. Approvals and purchasing commitments require their own defined workflow rather than being inferred from payment status.

An equipment purchase can be classified as a capital outflow rather than an operating expense. Payroll and tax payments require reviewed treatment and permissions. Category labels do not constitute formal accounting classifications.

## Budget model

One `fmg_budget` identifies year, branch/shared scope, and version. Statuses are proposed as Draft, Submitted, Approved, Rejected, or Superseded. Monthly allocations are `fmg_budgetallocation` rows and annual totals are derived.

- A `(budget version, month, leaf category)` allocation is unique.
- Approved versions and lines are immutable. Revising requires a new version and an explicit effective-from month.
- Never count two effective budget versions for the same month/scope/category or silently rewrite historical periods.
- Budget eligibility and flow-class mappings need an independently approved rule; they cannot be read from nonexistent Category columns.
- Only qualifying `Posted` expense payments consume budget actuals; unpaid commitments are not reserved in this model.
- Overspending is flagged. Blocking or overriding a payment requires explicit, server-enforced and concurrency-safe approval policy.

## Integrity and security

1. Posting requires authenticated actor, permitted branch/scope, valid payment date, positive PEN amount, active leaf category, reviewed expense class, and active authorized payment account.
2. `Posted` payments are immutable. Corrections require an authorized, justified void and a new payment; no deletion of posted history.
3. Draft and Voided rows are excluded from paid-spending measures.
4. Category `999`, group codes, and inactive categories cannot be posted.
5. Duplicate integration requests cannot create duplicate posted payments when an external idempotency key is introduced.
6. Approved budget versions and lines cannot be edited silently.
7. Cross-record checks, posting, voiding, and approvals must be enforced server-side across all clients. Power Fx or asynchronous Power Automate alone is insufficient.
8. Native Dataverse audit history and business event visibility have separate access and retention policies.
9. Power BI reporting may show paid spending, categories, branch and shared allocations, capital/operating classes, budget utilization, and historical differences. **It must not show revenue, profit, gross/net margins, net invoicing, complete net cash flow, or actual account balances** without a separate data model.
10. All report figures must reconcile to authorized source records and show dataset refresh timing.
